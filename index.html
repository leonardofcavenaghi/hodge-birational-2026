01 / Overview

About the Conference

This conference brings together specialists in Complex Algebraic Geometry and Hodge theory to present recent research, exchange methods, and foster collaboration within the international mathematical community.

A thematic focus of this edition is advancing the frontier of AI in Algebraic Geometry: examining how artificial intelligence, symbolic computation, and automated reasoning support conjecture formulation, explicit calculation, and proof verification.
